The value of the engineer now lies in defining product character, exercising taste, and determining which features align with specific user needs. This transition emphasizes the human role in curating software experiences rather than merely producing code.

**「Impact」** Engineers must prioritize developing product intuition and user-centric design skills to remain competitive, as technical implementation becomes a commodity that AI can perform more efficiently.

rss · Ai2 News · Oct 31, 00:00

**「Background」** AstaBrief serves as the underlying model for the &\#x27;Fast&\#x27; report-generation mode within the Asta research assistant, which previously relied on proprietary models like Claude for its &\#x27;Thinking&\#x27; mode. **「Impact」** Developers can now deploy a specialized, smaller-scale model for scientific summarization and citation tasks without relying on proprietary, closed-source APIs.
