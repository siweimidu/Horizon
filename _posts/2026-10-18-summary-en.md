### [Kyutai Releases REKI, a 2.4B Parameter Vision-Language-Action Model for Robotics](https://aihot.news/items/z10riaoda3rrn6m1q52k00rgh) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Kyutai, in collaboration with Inria and École des Ponts, has released REKI, a 2.4 billion parameter vision-language-action \(VLA\) model optimized for robot manipulation. The model was pre-trained exclusively on public data and is now available on Hugging Face under a CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license.

rss · AIHOT 全站 · Oct 17, 20:22

**「Background」** Vision-language-action models are a class of embodied AI architectures that integrate visual perception and linguistic instructions to generate direct motor control commands for robotic systems. These models aim to bridge the gap between high-level task understanding and low-level physical execution.

**「Impact」** The open-source release of REKI provides researchers and developers with a lightweight, 2.4B parameter alternative for testing and deploying robotic manipulation tasks without requiring proprietary or large-scale private datasets.
